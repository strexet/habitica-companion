Connect Codex or ChatGPT

  1. Start the connection in your client: Authenticate in Codex Settings → MCP servers, or Connect in ChatGPT. Keep the browser authorization page open.

Optional shared planning

Authenticated party planning

This queue is separate from legacy party planning. The server stores readable quest selections, contributor labels, votes and planning receipts. Only separately consented current party members can read shared planning. Records remain until explicit removal. Planning does not start quests, purchase scrolls or cast spells.

You can export or remove your own contributions after leaving a party. Removing your queued quests also removes votes on those quests. The verified current leader can delete the whole authenticated queue. Browser-claimed roles grant no access. Cloud workspace deletion and credential deletion do not remove these separate shared records; use the contribution controls before credential deletion or revalidate later.

Authenticated party planning is not enabled on this app.
